Beginner Bikes

  1. Sport Bike: Kawasaki Ninja 500

    The Kawasaki Ninja 500 is a beginner-friendly sport bike that combines smooth power, lightweight handling, and modern styling. It is powerful enough for highway riding while still being manageable for new riders learning motorcycle control.

    Pros:

    • Lightweight and easy to handle
    • Comfortable for daily riding
    • Good balance of power and control
    • Modern sport bike styling

    Cons:

    • Limited storage space
    • Forward riding position may feel uncomfortable on long rides
    • Insurance can be more expensive than other beginner bikes

    Click here to learn more about the Kawasaki Ninja 500.

  2. Cruiser Bike: Honda Rebel 500

    The Honda Rebel 500 is one of the most popular beginner cruiser motorcycles. It has a low seat height, relaxed riding position, and smooth engine performance, which helps new riders feel comfortable and confident.

    Pros:

    • Low seat height for easier balance
    • Comfortable riding position
    • Reliable and beginner-friendly
    • Easy to ride in traffic

    Cons:

    • Minimal wind protection
    • Smaller fuel tank than larger cruisers
    • Not designed for off-road riding

    Click here to learn more about the Honda Rebel 500.

  3. Adventure Bike: Honda NX500

    The Honda NX500 is a beginner-friendly adventure motorcycle designed for both everyday commuting and long-distance travel. It features a comfortable upright riding position, smooth engine performance, and the ability to handle different road and weather conditions.

    Pros:

    • Comfortable upright seating position
    • Great fuel economy
    • Good for commuting and touring
    • Smooth and beginner-friendly power delivery

    Cons:

    • Taller seat height may be difficult for shorter riders
    • Limited off-road capability compared to larger ADV bikes
    • Heavier than smaller beginner motorcycles

    Click here to learn more about the Honda NX500.

  4. Naked Bike: KTM 390 Duke

    The KTM 390 Duke is a lightweight naked motorcycle known for its sharp handling and fun riding experience. Its upright seating position and responsive controls make it a popular option for beginners who want a sporty feel without a full sport bike.

    Pros:

    • Very lightweight and easy to maneuver
    • Comfortable upright seating position
    • Modern technology features
    • Strong performance for its size

    Cons:

    • Minimal wind protection at highway speeds
    • Firm seat may become uncomfortable on long rides
    • Smaller size may not fit taller riders comfortably

    Click here to learn more about the KTM 390 Duke.

  5. Touring Bike: Kawasaki Versys 650

    The Kawasaki Versys 650 is a versatile touring motorcycle designed for comfort, commuting, and long-distance riding. It has an upright seating position, smooth engine performance, and enough power for highway travel.

    Pros:

    • Comfortable for long rides
    • Good wind protection
    • Strong and smooth engine performance
    • Practical for commuting and touring

    Cons:

    • Taller seat height for shorter riders
    • Heavier than smaller motorcycles
    • More expensive than entry-level bikes

    Click here to learn more about the Kawasaki Versys 650.
