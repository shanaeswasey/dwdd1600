Who were the Signers

Famous Signers

Some of the most famous signers of the United States Declaration of Independence include:

John Hancock
President of the Second Continental Congress; known for his large, bold signature, which became synonymous with the word “signature” itself.
Thomas Jefferson
Primary author of the Declaration of Independence and later the third U.S. president.
Benjamin Franklin
Founding Father, diplomat, inventor, and one of the oldest and most influential signers.
John Adams
Strong advocate for independence and later the second U.S. president.
Samuel Adams
Massachusetts revolutionary leader and major organizer of resistance against British rule.
Roger Sherman
The only person to sign all four major founding documents of the United States.
Robert Morris
Known as the “Financier of the American Revolution” for helping fund the Revolutionary War.
Charles Carroll
Charles Carroll of Carrollton; the only Catholic signer and one of the wealthiest men in the colonies.
Richard Henry Lee
Virginia statesman who introduced the resolution for independence in Congress.
Elbridge Gerry
Signer of the Declaration and later U.S. vice president; the term “gerrymandering” comes from his name.

The signers of the Declaration of Independence played a critical role in the founding of the United States. By signing the document, they openly supported the colonies' break from British rule and risked their reputations, property, and even their lives. Their actions helped establish the principles of liberty, self-government, and individual rights that continue to shape the nation today
